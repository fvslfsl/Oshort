Step syntax & tips
BookingsClick the menu, button or option with that visible text.
@Field label = valueType a value into the field with that label.
clear: Field labelDelete the text in that field.
key: EscapePress a key or combo, e.g. Enter, Ctrl+Enter.
… (delay 1s)Pause after a step. Also (delay 500ms).
# group: NameIn a .txt file, under the # Preset name line, sets the group.

Shortcuts: presets get Alt1…Alt9 by position unless you set a custom one (click the key caps). In OPERA, CtrlK opens the preset palette and EscEsc stops a running preset. Drag ⠿ to reorder presets or steps; drop a preset into another column to move it to that group.
